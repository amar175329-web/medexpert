# Open Research Items (must be resolved before public launch)

1. **Authoritative Indian medicine data source(s):** which official or licensed sources exist, API or download, licence terms, update frequency. Until decided, the catalog stays empty and every medicine is Unverified.
2. **Barcode/QR coverage:** what share of Indian packs and strips carry a usable code and in what standard.
3. **Free-tier limits and real costs:** Vercel (and its commercial-use terms), Supabase, Upstash, Sentry, Resend, and WhatsApp Cloud API per-message pricing and template approval. Map to user counts.
4. **Legal and privacy:** applicability of India's data protection law to health data, consent, retention, deletion, terms, disclaimer, liability for a public medical-information product.
5. **Emergency numbers:** confirm national emergency, ambulance and poison-control numbers from official sources; record the source in config.
6. **Disposal guidance:** cite an official Indian source.
7. **iPhone PWA limits:** reliability of push and offline reminders on current iOS; set user-facing guidance.
8. **Local model:** exact model name, size and hosting method; Hindi and medical-text quality; where the bridge will run in production.
9. **OCR quality** for Hindi and English medicine packs; decide whether Tesseract.js is enough or a better component is needed.
10. **Name and brand:** MedExpert is chosen; check trademark and domain availability. Also consider that "Expert" in a medical name may imply professional authority; the legal review should confirm the name is acceptable.
